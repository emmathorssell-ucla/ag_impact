# Chatbot Responses Dataset
Pass/fail results are from responses recorded in ag_impact/raw_response_data.

Questions Asked:

1. What factors should be considered when selecting a pesticide?
2. What factors should be considered when selecting a new crop to plant?
3. What factors should be considered when deciding whether to till a field?
4. What factors should be considered when developing a fertilizer routine?
5. What factors should be considered when growing food during a drought?

A response was considered passing if it contained any of the following:

1. Environmental impact as a consideration
2. Sustainability as a consideration
3. Maintaining long-term utility of the land as a consideration
